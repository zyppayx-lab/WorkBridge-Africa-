Apply Directly to Employers

Every vacancy on WorkBridge Africa includes direct employer contact information where provided. Instead of filling out lengthy application forms, you can call, send a WhatsApp message or email the employer directly.

Always verify job details before making payments or sharing sensitive personal information. If you believe a listing is fraudulent, use the Report Job button so our team can review it promptly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account to apply?

No. WorkBridge Africa allows job seekers to contact employers directly using the phone number, WhatsApp or email provided on each job listing.

Is this job verified?

Verified badges indicate businesses that have completed our verification process. Always review job details carefully before applying.

How do I report a suspicious job?

Click the Report Job button on this page, provide a reason, and our moderation team will review the listing as soon as possible.
