Find Jobs Across Africa

WorkBridge Africa connects job seekers with trusted businesses across Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, South Africa, Uganda and many other African countries. Browse jobs in technology, construction, hospitality, healthcare, education, engineering, logistics, fashion, agriculture and more.

Every job listing contains the employer's preferred contact information, including phone numbers, WhatsApp, and email addresses where available. This allows applicants to contact businesses directly without creating an account.

New vacancies are published daily, making WorkBridge Africa one of the fastest-growing employment directories focused on African businesses and professionals.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account to apply?

No. Job seekers do not need to register. Simply open the job and contact the employer using the provided phone number, WhatsApp or email.

Are the jobs verified?

Jobs are posted by registered businesses. If you notice suspicious activity, use the Report Job feature on the job details page.

How can my business post jobs?

Register a business account, create your business profile, then publish unlimited job listings from your dashboard.
